# User Scenario — Orbit v1

## The User

**Bharathi, Marketing Analyst**

Works at a marketing technology agency managing multiple D2C client brands simultaneously. She is responsible for weekly performance reporting, anomaly identification, and preparing context for client calls.

---

## The Situation

It's Monday morning. Bharathi has a client call at 11 AM for one of her brands — a D2C skincare label running paid campaigns on Meta and driving traffic to their Shopify store.

Before Orbit, this morning looked like this:

- Open Meta Ads and export last week's numbers  
- Open GA4 and note sessions and conversion events separately  
- Open Shopify and check revenue  
- Paste everything into a spreadsheet  
- Align date ranges manually  
- Notice that Meta and GA4 are showing different conversion numbers  
- Spend time figuring out why  
- Write a summary in slides before the client call  

By the time she's done, it's already close to the meeting and she's rushing.

---

## The Interaction with Orbit

**8:45 AM** — Bharathi opens Orbit and selects the skincare brand from the brand selector. She sets the date range to last week.

The dashboard loads. She sees ROAS, spend, sessions, and conversion rate in one unified view — all pulled from Meta, GA4, and Shopify during the overnight sync.

There's one anomaly flag:

> **ROAS dropped 18% week-over-week on paid campaigns**

The alert shows the threshold that triggered it and links directly to the channel breakdown.

She clicks into it.

Spend remained stable, but conversions dropped mid-week. The weekly summary confirms the change and highlights Thursday as the point where performance started declining.

Bharathi no longer needs to jump between multiple platforms or manually consolidate numbers into spreadsheets before understanding what changed.

By **9:10 AM**, she is already preparing talking points for the client call.

---

## What Orbit Did

- Reduced the reporting workflow from hours of manual consolidation to a few focused minutes of investigation  
- Surfaced the anomaly before Bharathi had to go looking for it  
- Provided consistent, traceable metrics she can confidently reference during the meeting  
- Reduced dependency on analyst-specific workflows and manual reporting habits  

---

## What Orbit Did NOT Do

Orbit did not tell Bharathi what decision to make.

It did not recommend a budget change.

It did not generate an AI summary she would have to second-guess.

Instead, Orbit provided fast, reliable, traceable information — and let the analyst do the thinking.

That's the point.
